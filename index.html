pressing-mattersIn progress

Do different pressings of the same album actually sound different? Everyone with a record collection has an opinion; almost nobody measures it. A BS.1770 loudness and dynamics implementation, validated against signals with analytically known answers, pointed at vinyl, CD, and streaming masters of the same records.

9 validation checks vs theory · 0 failures

▶ Analyse your own audio

Python · NumPy · SciPy · ITU-R BS.1770-4 · EBU R128

birdsong-robustnessIn progress

Published bird-song classifiers are evaluated on clean, curated recordings. The field is wind, traffic, overlapping calls, and cheap microphones. This measures how far they actually fall — a robustness benchmark across controlled degradation axes.

Python · TensorFlow · BirdNET · audio augmentation
